rustine

A small 2D pixel-art engine in Rust. Pick a game: the demo, or a tutorial.

  • DemoEverything at once: the blob, walls and crates, a trigger zone, music and sounds, a pause menu.Bottom half: stick (or WASD / arrows). Top half or Space: action. Pause button or P: pause. R: restart.
  • 1. Sprites & animationAn Aseprite sheet in the world, sprites and animators on entities, the core's animate and draw systems, draw order.Tap or Space: splosh once.
  • 2. InputRaw events forwarded by the engine, held keys, a touch stick, an anchored button, world pixels vs UI pixels.Drag in the bottom half or WASD / arrows: move. Corner button or Space: change color.
  • 3. ScenesA title, a play screen and a pause menu as a stack of scenes: per-scene systems, enter / exit / pause / resume hooks, entities scoped to a scene, a curtain transition.Title: tap or Space. Playing: top-right buttons or P (pause), R (restart), Esc / back (title). Paused: house button (title), tap elsewhere (resume).
  • 4. CollisionsCircle and box colliders, solids and triggers, layers and masks, detection as a system, contacts as events, the debug outlines.Drag anywhere (like a trackpad) or WASD / arrows: move the circle.
  • 5. Arcade vs rigid physicsThe same room moved by the core's two physics plugs, one per scene: kinematic push-out and slide, or mass, bounce, friction and impulses.Drag anywhere or WASD / arrows: drive into the crates. Top-right button or Space: switch physics. Shake button or R: kick the crates.
  • 6. AudioA sound board: sounds in memory or streamed, voices, buses, fades, pan, and variation rolled by the game's own random generator.Top zone or Space: a hit, panned where you tap. Bottom zones (or P, W, S): music on / off, duck the music, pause the effects.
  • 7. Saves & lifecycleA counter that remembers: a save as a serde struct (JSON), loaded at launch, stored on quit and when the app goes to the background, the on_background / on_foreground hooks.Tap or Space: count and change color. R: forget everything. Switch apps or lock the phone, then come back (F7 on desktop).
Esc / Android back quits a game; the ← list button comes back here.
